Component concentration:
    mol_fraction_i = F_i / F_total
    c_i = c_total * mol_fraction_i

Store carbon concentration in calc_block.

6.X NANOPARTICLE METAL ATOM COUNT (MANDATORY)
----------------------------------------------------------------
If **Average_size_M_nanoparticle** is provided, you MUST compute:

### (1) Convert diameter (nm) → radius (m)
r = (d / 2) × 1e-9

### (2) Nanoparticle volume
V = (4/3)π r³

### (3) Determine metal density
You MUST:
- Try to identify the metal element from catalyst composition
  (e.g., Ti, Fe, Co, Ni, Mo, W, Pt, etc.)
- Use the density of the **ELEMENTAL METAL**, not compound
  (e.g., Ti = 4500 kg/m³, Fe = 7874 kg/m³)

If metal element is unknown:
→ leave value empty ""
→ explain in calc_steps and notes.

### (4) Mass of nanoparticle
m = ρ × V

### (5) Moles of metal atoms
n = m / (M_element × 1e-3)
(M_element = atomic mass of the identified metal, e.g., Ti = 47.867 g/mol)

### (6) Metal atoms per nanoparticle
N_metal = n × N_A

Store as:
Metal_atoms_per_nanoparticle

### (7) Nanoparticle number concentration
n_NP = c_M / n

### (8) Carbon per nanoparticle
C_NP_ratio = c_C / n_NP

If any required value missing:
- return ""
- fully explain in calc_steps

SCORING RULES (STRICT)
================================================================
Determine the score ONLY from explicit textual descriptions found in the paper.

================================================================
THE 10 D-MODE SCORING FACTORS
================================================================

1. Formation quality of floating catalyst nanoparticles
   - explicit formation of stable Fe/Co/Ni nanoparticles = 1
   - partially described decomposition = 0.5
   - unclear or missing = 0

2. Transport efficiency of nanoparticles toward the substrate
   - clear gas-phase transport / well-dispersed NPs = 1
   - partial transport or aggregation mentioned = 0.5
   - no evidence of effective transport = 0

3. Successful landing / deposition of nanoparticles on substrate
   - explicit NP landing, nucleation on substrate = 1
   - partial or inconsistent = 0.5
   - unclear or minimal deposition = 0

4. Nucleation behavior on the substrate
   - clear CNT nucleation from NP/substrate interface = 1
   - partial nucleation = 0.5
   - not described = 0

5. CNT film uniformity or pattern quality
   - dense/uniform/random network clearly described = 1
   - moderate uniformity = 0.5
   - sparse or poorly described = 0

6. CNT purity and structural quality (textual)
   - high purity, low amorphous carbon, graphitized = 1
   - moderate quality = 0.5
   - high defects or unclear = 0

7. Hydrocarbon/metal ratio suitability (textual coherence)
   - balanced decomposition / proper ratio = 1
   - partially reasonable = 0.5
   - excessive carbon or poisoning = 0

8. Growth stability and repeatability
   - stable multi-minute or multi-hour growth = 1
   - some stability = 0.5
   - unstable / unclear = 0

9. Evidence of controlled morphology (vertical, random mat, etc.)
   - explicit form (aligned mat, uniform mat, etc.) = 1
   - partial morphology = 0.5
   - undefined = 0

10. Process clarity and completeness in paper
    - detailed reactor → catalyst → substrate growth chain = 1
    - partially described mechanism = 0.5
    - unclear, fragmented, or incomplete = 0
