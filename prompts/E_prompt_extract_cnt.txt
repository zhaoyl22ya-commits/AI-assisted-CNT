PART 1 — NUMERIC RANGE RULE (MANDATORY)
================================================================
Whenever you encounter a numeric range such as:
 - 700–900 °C
 - 30–120 min
 - 500–1500 sccm
 - 5–15 nm

You MUST:
1. Identify min and max.
2. Compute midpoint = (min + max)/2.
3. Use ONLY the midpoint as the extracted numeric value.
4. Keep the appropriate normalized units (see PART 5).

================================================================

ALLOWED (CNT GROWTH) examples:

- Direct spinning / sock formation / fiber drawing growth zones

FORBIDDEN (MUST BE IGNORED) examples:
- Catalyst precursor synthesis routes
- Catalyst calcination, reduction, pre-treatment, annealing

- Furnace tube baking, cleaning, or conditioning

PRESSURE RULE:

6.2 Concentrations (mol/m³)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Use ideal gas law:
    c_total = P / (R * T_K)
    T_K = T_C + 273.15
    R = 8.314 J/mol/K

Component concentration:
    mol_fraction_i = F_i / F_total
    c_i = c_total * mol_fraction_i

For EACH synthesis_N, compute:
 - carbon concentration

Store them in synthesis_N.calc_block.

----------------------------------------------------------------
6.3 Catalyst Atomic Composition
----------------------------------------------------------------
If catalyst has multiple metals:
 atomic%_i = (n_i / Σ n_j) × 100

If paper only provides weight%:
 convert weight% → mol% using atomic mass.

Store the final atomic % in the "catalysts" field of the corresponding synthesis_N.
