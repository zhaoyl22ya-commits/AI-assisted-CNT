PART 9 — FLUIDIZED-BED CVD CNT QUALITY SCORING (MODE G ONLY)
================================================================

After extracting all CNT growth parameters, the system MUST evaluate the
quality and effectiveness of this fluidized-bed CNT growth process (Mode G).

================================================================
SCORING RULES (STRICT AND MANDATORY)
================================================================
Determine the score ONLY from explicit textual descriptions in the paper.
Do NOT invent numbers. Do NOT assume reactor geometry, Umf, density, or
CNT dimensions unless explicitly stated.

Each factor contributes:
- 1 point  = strong explicit evidence
- 0.5 point = partial or implied
- 0 point = unclear / absent / contradictory

1. **Quality of fluidization regime**
   - explicitly “stable fluidization”, “uniform bubbling/turbulent regime” = 1
   - partially stable = 0.5
   - no or poor fluidization description = 0

2. **Correct operation relative to minimum fluidization velocity (Umf)**
   - gas velocity ≈ (1–5×Umf) clearly stated = 1
   - somewhat consistent or implied = 0.5
   - no Umf mention / unclear = 0

3. **Catalyst dispersion quality in the bed**
   - well-dispersed catalyst or FCC/Al2O3 bed = 1
   - partially dispersed or agglomeration noted = 0.5
   - severe agglomeration / unclear = 0

4. **CNT yield or productivity**
   - high yield, heavy CNT coating, thick powder = 1
   - moderate yield = 0.5
   - not described = 0

5. **CNT purity and defect level (textual)**
   - high purity, graphitized, smooth walls = 1
   - moderate quality = 0.5
   - unclear / defective = 0

6. **Hydrodynamic stability during reaction**
   - smooth operation over long time / no bed collapse = 1
   - partially stable = 0.5
   - unstable bed / slugging / collapse = 0

7. **Uniformity of CNT growth on bed particles**
   - uniform CNT coating across particles or agglomerates = 1
   - partially uniform = 0.5
   - heterogeneous or unclear = 0

8. **Efficiency of hydrocarbon conversion**
   - text indicates effective decomposition / conversion = 1
   - partially effective = 0.5
   - poor or unspecified = 0

9. **Reactor-scale consistency**
   - reproducible across multiple runs OR scalable to large bed = 1
   - implied reproducibility = 0.5
   - no reproducibility = 0

10. **Clarity and completeness of fluidized-bed operating details**
    - operating regime, gas flows, bed temp, feed mode all clearly described = 1
    - partially described = 0.5
    - missing key info = 0

================================================================
FINAL SCORING METHOD
================================================================
TotalScore = sum(all 10 factors)
Round TotalScore to nearest integer (1–10).

Insert into JSON:

"growth_quality_score_G": ""
"quality_evidence": "Explain which textual cues support the score."
