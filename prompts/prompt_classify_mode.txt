===============================
CNT GROWTH MODE DEFINITIONS
===============================

A = Floating-catalyst CVD producing macroscopic CNT bulk, fibers, films, socks, or aerogels.
    (Catalyst injected into gas phase; CNTs form free-standing macroscopic products.)

B = Substrate-supported CVD vertical aligned CNT arrays (VAF).
    (Catalyst not injected into gas phase but loaded on the substrate before the CNT growth;
     CNTs grow vertically aligned.)

C = Substrate-supported CVD horizontal aligned CNT arrays (HAF).
    (Catalyst not injected into gas phase but loaded on the substrate before the CNT growth;
     CNTs grow horizontally aligned across substrate.)

D = Floating-catalyst CVD with catalyst deposition onto substrate.
    (Catalyst is generated in gas phase as floating nanoparticles and then
     deposits onto the substrate, where CNTs subsequently nucleate and grow
     on the substrate surface.)

E = Substrate-supported catalyst CVD but NO aligned CNT array forms.
    (Catalyst not injected into gas phase but loaded on the substrate before the CNT growth;
     Random CNT mats, disordered CNT films, powders, entangled networks, carpet-like but non-aligned.)

===============================
EXTENDED CVD MODES (F–H)
===============================

F = Plasma-Enhanced CVD (PECVD).
    (CNTs grown under plasma excitation: RF plasma, microwave plasma,
     bias voltage, ion bombardment, low-temperature CNT growth.
     Terms such as "PECVD", "plasma-enhanced", "RF plasma", "microwave PECVD",
     "ion-assisted CNT growth", "bias-assisted CNT growth" MUST be classified as F.)

G = Fluidized-bed / Floating-bed powder CVD.
    (Large-scale CNT powders produced in fluidized reactors:
     fluidization gas velocity, floating catalyst particles,
     CNT powders, carbon nanofibers produced in fluidized beds.
     Terms such as "fluidized bed", "fluidization", "floating bed reactor",
     "CNT powder production", "mass production CNT powders" MUST be classified as G.)

H = CO/HiPco high-pressure CVD (CO disproportionation CNT growth).
    (Fe(CO)5 decomposition, high-pressure CO (5–50 bar),
     CO → C + CO2 disproportionation, HiPco reactor systems.
     Terms such as "HiPco", "CO disproportionation", "Fe(CO)5",
     "high-pressure CO CVD", "CO-based CNT growth" MUST be classified as H.)

===============================
NON-CVD / UNCATEGORIZED METHODS (I)
===============================

I = All CNT synthesis methods that are NOT A–H.
    This includes NON-CVD methods such as:
    - Arc discharge CNT synthesis
    - Laser ablation CNT synthesis
    - Molten-salt CVD CNT synthesis
    - Hydrothermal CNT / solvothermal CNT
    - Template-based CNT growth (AAO template CNT)
    - Electrochemical CNT synthesis
    - Ball-milling + annealing carbon nanotubes
    - Biological/biotemplate CNT synthesis
    - Any other method not matching A–H

===============================
CLASSIFICATION RULES
===============================

1. If catalyst is not injected into gas phase but loaded on the substrate before the CNT growth, and the CNT product show:
   - vertical alignment → classify as B.
   - horizontal alignment → classify as C.

2. If catalyst is injected into the gas phase (floating catalyst) and CNT product is:
   - macroscopic fiber / sock / aerogel → classify as A.
   - growth on the substrate forming aligned arrays → classify as D.

3. If catalyst is not injected into gas phase but loaded on the substrate before the CNT growth,
   and CNTs are NOT vertically or horizontally aligned:
   → classify as E.

4. If plasma-related terms appear ("PECVD", "plasma", "RF plasma", "microwave plasma",
   "bias-assisted", "ion bombardment"):
   → classify as F (PECVD).

5. If fluidized-bed, fluidization velocity, CNT powders from fluidized reactors:
   → classify as G.

6. If CO/HiPco high-pressure carbon monoxide disproportionation appears:
   → classify as H.

7. If none of A–H apply, or if the method is arc discharge, laser ablation,
   hydrothermal, template growth, electrochemical, mechanochemical,
   or any non-CVD method:
   → classify as I.

8. If information is unclear or conflicting → return "".
